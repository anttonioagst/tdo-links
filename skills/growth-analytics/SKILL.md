# Growth Analytics

Optimize for qualified clicks until commission reports are integrated.

## Daily Review

Analyze:

- Offers with most clicks.
- Offers with zero clicks after publishing.
- Category and price bands that outperform.
- Telegram vs X contribution.
- Copy patterns associated with higher engagement.

## Recommendations

Make recommendations as reversible rules first. Do not silently change scoring thresholds unless the same pattern repeats across multiple cycles.

## Report Shape

Return:

- Period.
- Conclusions.
- Suggested changes.
- Expected impact.
- Top clicked offers.
